How to play Ward Strain Spotters
  1. Each scenario shows a real situation from the ward strain systematic review — sometimes from a staff perspective only, sometimes staff and patient side by side.
  2. Read the situation, then look at What to classify — the specific action you need to sort.
  3. Sort it two ways: the SPO layer (Structure, Process, or Outcome) and the resilience capacity (Respond, Monitor, Learn, or Anticipate).
  4. Drag a chip into a box, or tap a chip then tap a box — both work.
  5. Changed your mind? Tap the × on a filled box to clear it and pick again.
  6. Once both boxes are filled, tap Check. You'll see what's right, what's not, and why — plus the study it's drawn from.
  7. Tap Next to continue, or Back to revisit a previous scenario — your answer and feedback are saved.
  8. At the end, discuss as a table: what is one thing your ward could do differently?

These scenarios are drawn directly from a mixed methods systematic review of 16 studies on hospital ward strain. The evidence shows that ward strain is a systemic issue — not an individual failing — and that staff predominantly use reactive resilience to cope, with little anticipation or learning built into the system.
